## Later-work screen and limits

Execution searches included `partial order copula density estimation`, `causal order density confidence reconstruction`, `copula density confidence bands uniform 2025 2026`, and the cited wavelet titles. Results were checked on primary sites; search freshness labels were not treated as publication dates. The search followed the 2024 paper back to Seck–Mamane's complete 2023 preprint.

[Boubaker–Belgacem (2025), Mathematics 13(24), 3932](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7390/13/24/3932), §3.4, explicitly forms paired pseudo-observations from coordinate samples and applies iterative thresholding. This screened later example does not remove our observation mismatch; no theorem from it is imported. A 2026 search hit on covariate-dependent **parametric** copula likelihood also concerns another experiment. Neither is evidence of an exact S031 solution. This is not an exhaustive citation census.

## Claim-by-claim result

| Proposed contribution | Decision before implementation |
| --- | --- |
| A new copula density estimator merely because it uses ranks | Rejected as a novelty claim. |
| New coordinate recoverability or qualitative forcing | Rejected; Winkler precedents apply. |
| Finite, order-only simultaneous cell-mass restrictions with explicit calibration and exact outer certificates | Worth the scoped feasibility test. The composition must prove one orientation, latent-cell concentration, cutoff selection and conservative failure. Useful density widths are still an independent requirement. |
| Two-point smooth marginal-preserving minimax obstruction | Verify inside K; classify as a standard transferred density-estimation lower bound unless an additional order-specific separation is proved. |
| Matching optimal rates or an extra price for losing ranks | Not established by the inspected sources or this candidate. Would require more work. |

The local-mass route therefore survives the mathematical implementation gate as an adaptation to assess, **not** as a demonstrated substantial new statistical result. The [decision report](finite-data-feasibility-decision.md) determines whether its finite widths justify continuation.
