# Version 0.1.0 finite causal order rank rigidity

This release packages the complete Lean proof of the finite realizer rank theorem and a draft preprint deriving an explicit inverse stability bound in a restricted two-dimensional conformal class.

Every two-order realizer of a grid-occupied sample admits one global exchange of axes giving both interior rank errors at most `30rn`, under the stated marginal and boundary hypotheses. Both final exports depend only on `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.

The full coefficient bound `d_conf <= 100 (N^(-1/12) + Delta_N)`, its probability argument, and its continuum/proper-time consequences are prose proofs. They are not wholly Lean verified. Novelty and independent review remain unconfirmed; no new physical law, curvature estimate or carrying-capacity gain is claimed.
