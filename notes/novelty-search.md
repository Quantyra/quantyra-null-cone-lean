# Novelty assessment of finite causal order reconstruction

2026-10-06. Quantyra Space E002/S005, requested by Dan. Verdict: no matching theorem located in the inspected sources; novelty remains provisional. This is a literature assessment, not a certificate that no prior result exists. Independent mathematical review has not occurred.

## Claims being assessed

The principal candidate contribution is the explicit uniform bound `d_conf <= 100 (N^(-1/12) + Delta_N)` on the specified smooth, positive, 2-Lipschitz, uniform-marginal densities on the square. Observations are iid finite chronological partial-order laws, with no coordinate rankings. Error is supremum density difference modulo exchange of the two axes. The constant and exponent themselves are not sufficient evidence of novelty: a stronger existing theorem, or an immediate specialization, would also defeat the claim.

The second candidate is the deterministic occupied-grid theorem: for EVERY two-order realizer there is ONE global swap making BOTH rank errors at every interior event at most `30rn`, under the stated occupancy, marginal and boundary conditions. This theorem is Lean verified. Formal verification establishes its correctness in the encoded assumptions; it does not establish originality.

All-size identifiability, orientation propagation, concentration, copula rank construction, and the proper-time corollary should be presented as background, established tools, specializations, or consequences. A novelty claim for the general slogan that causal order and volume determine geometry would be unsupported.
