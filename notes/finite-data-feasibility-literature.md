The execution comparison must also locate primary sources for the chosen finite Bernoulli/variance-sensitive bound, testing-to-estimation reduction, divergence data processing and any proposed sup-norm minimax packing. These are established tools, not claimed original contributions. Record the exact statement actually used, its probability normalization, loss, constants and assumptions. The scope's proof blueprints do not constitute imported theorem verification.

For every candidate novelty claim record: (1) closest theorem; (2) observation model; (3) density class and symmetry; (4) risk/confidence notion; (5) boundary and finite-n conditions; (6) algorithm/checker obligations; (7) what is inherited; (8) what remains to prove. Search citations and later work at execution time, retaining query/date/disposition rather than claiming absence from a few searches. A full comparison could conclude that the statistical claim is already known; that is an acceptable feasibility outcome.

The bounded comparison centers on recovering density from a two-dimensional partial order. General causal reconstruction up to isometry, higher-dimensional coordinate gauges, and Bayesian inference of latent orders from noisy rankings are different observation/target problems. Include them only when a theorem actually bears on this scope.
